- **The brand** (October 2026): the site read as generated: a purple-black ground, purple glows and blurred backdrops behind every section, gradient text in the headlines, a blinking cursor in the hero, small spaced capitals above every heading, numbered steps, crop marks around the windows and a glass nav. The redesign keeps every page, anchor, nav label, text and number, and changes the identity:
  - **The mark** is the corner and cursor the favicon and the TUI's welcome screen already drew: the corner with its joint left open (the corner case) in cobalt, the cursor in ink, on a 12-unit grid (`Mark.astro`, `src/assets/logo-light.svg` and `logo-dark.svg` for Starlight, `public/favicon.svg` on a dark tile). The thin 4×4 corner that was the nav's logo is gone. The wordmark is `cornercase` in the display face.
  - **One accent**: cobalt `#005fff`, which is xterm colour 27, so the app (`BRAND_COLOR`) and the site draw the same brand. Blue because the TUI already gives yellow, orange, red, green and cyan to working, waiting, failed, done and the selection; purple stays only on merged pull requests, as on GitHub. Text links use `--brand-ink` (`#0050d8` light, `#74a3ff` dark) for contrast. Greys are neutral, not tinted.
  - **Light and dark** follow `prefers-color-scheme` on the landing, `/plugins/` and the docs; `/stats/` stays dark, since its ticket is a dark object. One theme per page, no section flips. Terminal pictures (the demo, the Why captures, the flow diagram, the docs screenshots) stay dark in both: they are the product's photographs, and dark slabs on the light page are the page's strongest contrast.
  - **Type**: Archivo, one variable family with a width axis (`@fontsource-variable/archivo/wdth.css`): titles at 112% (`--wide`), heavy and tight; text at 100%. JetBrains Mono only for code, commands, numbers that tick like a clock and terminal pictures. Bricolage Grotesque and Geist went: Geist is the template default of every new Next.js app.
  - **Shape**: square corners everywhere (buttons, boxes, inputs, code blocks, the docs' cards and search), like terminal cells; no shadows or glows; surfaces part by a tone step or a 1 px line, groups by a 2 px ink rule. A field to type in takes a darker edge (`--line-field`, `--sl-color-gray-4` in the docs) and a placeholder in the third grey, so both keep the contrast WCAG asks of a control (3:1) and of text (4.5:1) in both themes.
  - **The corner as a device**: once in the hero, two cobalt bars outside the demo window's top-left with the joint open, and once large in the closing section, beside `Give every case its own corner.`. Nowhere else, so it stays a signature rather than an ornament.
  - **Removed**: labels above headings and the numbered steps (each heading stands alone; the i18n keys went with them), gradient text, glows, blurred backdrops and dot grids, crop marks, window dots, the blinking cursor, pulsing "live" dots, the glass nav and its scroll listener (a plain sticky bar now), the hero's meta strip (its facts are one line above the headline) and the unicode glyphs that stood in for icons.
  - **Layout**: the hero is left-aligned, the install box beside the pitch so it shows on the first screen at 1440×900, and the demo keeps its exact stage width (`--g`, the old gutter), since its posters are cut to the live window's columns. Why is two split rows, then review and sessions side by side on a subgrid, then the agents band split: never more than two split rows in a row. The built-in tiles after the hero are a 3×3 grid of a bold name and one line, parted by 1 px lines (two columns below 900 px, one below 600); the feature list near the end is three grouped clusters with sticky titles instead of three columns of 18 glyph items. Flows' four points are a 2×2 with a cobalt rail.
  - **Motion** keeps what explains something (the demo and its tour, the race, the squares, the flow run, the sessions figure, entrances) and drops the decorative loops.
