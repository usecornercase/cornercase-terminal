#844 made bots with no face but `cornercase bot …`. The window draws a bot **as a chat** (bubbles, Markdown, what it is doing), never as the agent's interface, since #844 runs the agent without it and keeps the chat in `chat.jsonl`.

## The page

Bots are **a place of their own** (asked for on 2026-11-22, as the GPUI window had done that morning in its #401). Until then they were a section of the sidebar, below the projects and workspaces lists and above the Agents section, shown only once a bot existed: with none nothing said the window had bots, and with some they sat between two lists they have nothing to do with.

- **The button**: ` Bots › ` at the right of the Projects title row, in every layout (side by side, stacked, tree, compact's projects menu), whenever the window keeps bots (`App::bots_offered`: the server's bots folder is set, which a server does at start, or a bot is listed), so a newcomer finds them before making one. Before the label it shows the spinner while any bot thinks and the accent `●N` unread answers of every bot (`●9+` from ten; `ui::marks_width`), the marks the folded section's title had. They are part of the button, so a click on them opens the page, and are dropped, ` Bots › ` staying, when they would not fit beside the `Projects` title (`ui::layout::bots_button`, which the drawing follows), so they never cover it. Why there: compact mode had the same button in that row, the row exists in every layout and with the icon bar, so no row is added, and the chevron reads as a place to go, like ` Agents › `. Rejected: an icon in the icon bar (off by default, and it holds panels and tools), the footer (` More ▴ ` is the app's, the tools' grid is a project's), and keeping the section beside a button (two ways to the same rows).
- **What it draws** (`Bots::page`, `Areas::with_bots`): the projects section, title and list, goes to the bots (`Areas::bots`, `bots_title`, `bots_list`) in every wide layout, and `sidebar`, `title` and `list` are emptied, so drawing and hit testing need no case of their own; the layout is computed without the Agents section while the page shows (`Screen::wide`), so the list takes its room too, and its line cannot be dragged. The page is about bots: an agent that needs you still notifies, since no pane is on screen. The workspaces column (side by side) or section (stacked) shows the chosen bot's **card**, and nothing in the lobby; the tree has no card, as before. The footer, the icon bar, the tab bar and the pane's rect stay (PTYs keep their size). The title row is ` ‹ Projects ` then `Bots`; the rows are as before (the `bot list` order, the spinner or `●N`, the name, the detail, ` ⋯ ` on hover, no ` × `), then a sticky `+ New`, as under the projects and the workspaces, which opens the form. The section's ` + ` and its fold left with it: one New where the other lists have theirs, and nothing to fold on a page of its own.
- **Opening**: ` Bots › ` shows the chat of the bot shown last in this run of the server if it is still listed, else the first bot's, else the lobby (`App::open_bots`), so the page opens on something to read. In compact mode it opens the page's list (`Nav::Bots`) with no chat chosen, or the lobby when there is no bot, since a list of nothing explains nothing. A click on a row, a toast's ` go `, a desktop notification and ` create ` in the form show a chat and so open the page.
- **The lobby** (`ui/bots/lobby.rs`, `App::lobby`) is the main area while the page shows no chat. With no bot: `Meet your bots`, what a bot is, that it sets itself up from what it is for, schedules its own work and answers here (this server sets up every bot made with For, #887), ` + New bot `, and three ideas (PRs, News, Releases) whose click opens the form with Name and What it is for filled in (`open_bot_idea`), so a first bot is two clicks away. With bots: `Your bots`, a line to pick one in the list, and ` + New bot `. The ideas wrap beside a column of names and fill on hover; a low pane cuts them from the bottom. There is no case for "cannot list or make bots": the window is the server's, and the server always keeps its folder; the GPUI window's gate is for an older server or command line.
- **Leaving**: ` ‹ Projects ` (`App::press_back`) and every move that leaves a chat (below). **Removing the shown bot leaves its chat for the lobby** (`App::leave_chat`), not for the panes: the person was on the page and stays there. Opening the page leaves a machine's workspace, as showing a chat does, so ` ‹ Projects ` comes back to this computer's place; keeping the machine's place to come back to was left out.
- **Keys**: in the lobby no field has them and none reaches a pane (`forward_key` and `handle_paste` return early while the page shows). `Esc` closes a side panel when one shows and otherwise does nothing, as in the chat: leaving on `Esc` would give it two meanings on one page and drop the place on a stray key. The prefix key opens the keys menu over the page; its moves (tabs, workspaces, projects, panes, `a`, and the entries that act on the tab, `acts_here`) leave the page for that place, as they leave a chat; `b` opens the form over it; panels open beside it. No entry opens the page: `b` is new bot, and a keyboard user could not pick a row without the mouse anyway.
- **A click on a project or a workspace** cannot happen while the page shows in a wide layout, which draws neither list; the search's results, a tab of the tab bar (`tabs: top`) and an agent's notification go to their place and leave the page. In compact mode ` ‹ Projects ` on the list leaves the page for the projects menu, ` ≡ ` and `Esc` close the list on the page's chat or lobby, and ` ≡ ` on the page opens the list.
- **Nothing new on the socket.** The page is where this window draws the bots, like the compact menus (`nav`), which nothing reports either: its rows, unread counts and thinking are `bot list` and the `bot` events, the chosen chat is `shown_bot`, and the lobby holds no state of its own, so another UI builds its page from what is there (the GPUI window did). A program cannot tell the lobby from the panes: `status` keeps marking the local place `(shown)`, as under a compact menu, since `shown` names where the window goes back to. A request to open the page was rejected: no client needs it, and the one state a program acts on, the shown chat, is reported.
- **Not for the terminal**: the GPUI window's faces (the TUI draws none) and its new form.

## Where the chat shows, and what leaves it

- **In place of the panes.** A click on a bot's row shows its chat in `Areas::pane`, on the page, and the workspaces column shows the bot's **card**. The tab bar stays above (`tabs: top`), the panes keep their size (`App::layout` does not change), and a click on a tab goes back. `App::tab_view` returns `None`, `App::visible_tab` and `App::shows` say no pane is on screen, so agents in the hidden tab notify as in any other hidden tab, and PTY output does not redraw for nothing.
- **What leaves it**: every move to a local place calls `App::leave_remote`, which now also calls `leave_bot` (the chat and the page): a click on a project, a workspace, a tab or an agent row, `focus`, a notification click on an agent, the keys menu's moves, the picker and the search. Picking a machine's workspace (`select_on`) leaves it too. Showing a bot leaves a machine's workspace the same way. A bot being removed leaves it and closes any dialog naming that bot.
- **Shared by every window, not by views, and not saved.** The shown place is `App` state mirrored by every attached window (`design/client-server.md`), so the shown bot and the page are too (`Bots::shown`, `Bots::page`). A view (`cornercase view`) keeps a selection of its own and never shows a bot. After a restart the window shows the panes, as it does for a machine's workspace (`shown_remote`).
- **On the socket**: `Report.shown_bot` (feature `shown-bot`), not a field of `Ids` or `surface`: see `design/commands-for-scripts-and-agents/status.md`.

## The list

Bots keep their `bot list` order, not their status (rows that move are hard to click), and there is **no icon in the icon bar**, which holds panels and tools, while ` Bots › ` is in every layout's title row. The compact page is in [Layout](../layout.md).

## The chat

- **Lines**: only the shown bot's last 1,000 lines are in memory (`Chat.lines`), read when the chat is shown and then appended by `bot_append`; `cornercase bot history` reaches older ones. A `you` line is a bubble on the surface colour, right-aligned, at most 80 % of the width, with its time or `held` while its id is in `Live.held`. A `bot` line has a dim `name · time` above its Markdown (`highlight::markdown::render`, code blocks highlighted as in the files panel). A run of steps folds to its last 3 under a ` +N steps ` that a click unfolds (kept per chat by the run's first step). An error is red with ` retry ` when it is the last line and no turn runs, which sends the last `you` text again, or runs the setup again (`bot setup`) when the last line that opened a turn is `setting up`; another window does the same with the `setup` action, having no `you` line to send. A notice is centred. While a turn runs the last row is the spinner, `thinking · 42s · 3 steps` and ` stop `, and `stopping…` after a stop.
- **Rendering cost**: rows are laid out per entry from the newest, only as far as the view needs (`scroll + height`), and the Markdown of each answer is cached per line id and width (`Chat.bodies`, cleared when the width changes). A 60 fps draw never lays out the whole chat. The answer's code fences are highlighted ahead, in the turn's job (`markdown::warm`), so a first paint never runs the highlighter on the server's loop; a chat loaded by `show_bot` is not warmed (not measured).
- **The view** is anchored at the bottom; `Chat.scroll` counts the rows above it and 0 sticks. A line that arrives while it is above 0 adds its height to `scroll` so the text read stays put, and sets the ` ↓ new ` mark, which a click or reaching the bottom clears. The wheel scrolls as in a pane (`design/scrollback.md`).
- **The empty state** (a chat with no lines, which is a bot made without For: a setup's notice is a line) says what a bot is in two lines and offers three examples; a click fills the field and sends nothing. None promises a schedule (#846 adds that example).
- **Times** use the window's zone (`App::window_zone`, the queue form's): `HH:MM` today, else `Mon 3 Nov 14:02` (`queue::clock::when`, shared with `cornercase bot history`).
- **The header** has ` routines ` one cell left of ` new conversation ` (10 cells, cyan, enabled while a turn runs); it opens the bot's routines dialog (see [Bot routines](routines.md)). It is left out when keeping it would leave the name under 12 cells, and ` ⋯ ` still offers `routines…`.
- **` new conversation `** while a turn runs is dim, and a click says `PRs is answering: stop it first` (#844's `busy` refusal); the menu greys it.
- **The card** (workspaces column): the name, agent, model (`the agent's own` when unset), effort and account when set, the access (`full access` in red), `made 2026-11-14`; the agents it started that are still running (`started`, see Followed agents below); the folder comes right after the name, since the stacked layout gives the card few rows. Its click targets are the folder (a click opens the open-in menu, or copies the path), the `started` rows (a click shows that tab) and the two rows of a routine (a click opens its routines dialog, selected; the value turns cyan while hovered; ` ⋯ ` on its first row, or a right click on either, opens the routine's menu, see [Bot routines](routines.md)). The tree layout has no workspaces column, so it has no card.

## Followed agents in the window (#847)

The chat draws `started`, `finished` and `waiting` lines (`ui/bots/agent.rs`) as one entry each: a header with the glyph in the status colour, the text and ` show ` (cyan, `RowHit::Show`, which leaves the chat through `jump_to_pane`) or a dim `closed` for a pane that is gone or a line from another run of the server; under a `finished` its last message as Markdown (cached by line id like answers) and under a `waiting` the screen excerpt as plain text, both dim and indented, six rows and then ` +N lines ` (a click unfolds, keyed by the line id). The card lists the bot's live tagged agents under `started`, with the status icon, and a click on a row shows that tab (`card_row_at`). `by <bot>` is in the details line of tab rows, the tab bar and agent rows. The form's **Follow** row is a `[x]` checkbox like the queue form's cleanup (`Space` or a click toggles it) and makes the dialog 20 rows high. See [Following](following.md). The simulation's canned chat shows a started and a finished pair; ` show ` does nothing there.

## Routines in the window (#846)

A routine run's lines are drawn as one block, the bot's row shows `next 09:00` while the bot is idle, the card lists the routines, the empty state has a fourth example and the remove dialog names the entries it removes: see [Bot routines](routines.md) (The window). The simulation shows a canned run block and a seeded routine, and runs none; a bot made in its form with For filled in plays a canned setup that schedules a routine. Its bots' entries live in `app.botQueued`, not `app.queued`: the landing's scripted worlds and `queue.test.ts` need an empty project queue, while the card, the sidebar's `next` and the routines dialog need the bot's routine in them too. `pauseQueued`, `removeQueued` and `removal` take the store to act on, so the dialog reuses the queue modal's code and its undo toast, and the card's routine menu shares the dialog's (`actOnRoutine` in the simulation).

## Keys

While a chat shows, every key goes to its input field and **never to a pane**: `chat_typing` is true when a bot shows, no compact menu or full-screen file is up, and the TODO field, the files search, the changes filter and the tour do not have the keys (they keep them). `forward_key` and `handle_paste` also return early while a bot shows, so no path can write to a hidden pane. The prefix key still opens the keys menu. `Enter` sends (any modifiers); a line break comes only from a paste (`Editor::insert_lines`); the reason is in `design/text-fields.md`. `Esc` clears a draft with text; with an empty one it closes a side panel when one shows, and otherwise does nothing: going back to the last tab would give `Esc` two meanings depending on the text, and a stray `Esc` would drop the place you are reading. The field is `TextField::BotChat` in the registry, so drags, double clicks and the copy/cut/select-all menu work as in every field. Drafts are per bot and shared by windows, like every field in `App`.

## Unread and ` go `

- An answer or error of a bot whose chat a window shows on screen (`bot_on_screen`: shown, a window attached, no compact menu, no full-screen file, or a visible view that names the bot: `view --bot`, #869, see Commands for scripts and agents › Views) neither counts as unread nor notifies; showing a chat does what `bot seen` does. Any other keeps #844's toast and desktop notification, which now carry a token (`Notices::bot_token`, bounded like the agents' tokens): the toast's ` go ` and a click on the desktop notification show the chat, or toast `that bot is gone`. `act_on_notice` and `cornercase go` are unchanged: a bot's token is not published on `events`, whose `bot` events already carry its lines.

## The form, rename and remove

- **The form** (`bots/form.rs` is the model, `ui/dialog/bot.rs` the drawing): Name, Agent, Model, Effort, Account, Access, Follow and What it is for. The agent list is `bot agents`' (`bot_agent_infos`), with the agents that cannot be bots greyed with their `why` (see Dialogs › Disabled options and typed values); the preselected one is `config.agent` when it can be a bot. The model is a combobox that also takes a typed name, `default` meaning the agent's own. Effort shows only for an agent that takes one; Account only when the agent has accounts, as in the queue form (`AgentAccounts`), with the bot's own login preselected when editing. **Access is never chosen for the user when `cornercase` is not offered** (Codex today): ` create ` answers `choose its access` under the form without sending. The note under it has two lines, the full access one in the error colour. ` create ` shows the chat, where a bot made with For sets itself up (#887), and ` save ` of a changed For runs nothing. Server refusals (name taken, 50 bots) show under the form, which stays open; any key clears them.
- **`edit…`** is the same form with ` save `, the agent grey and skipped by `Tab`. It sends `about` only when the text changed, through `EditBot.about` (contract feature `bot-about`; `bot edit --about TEXT` for programs, checked by the CLI before sending), which writes `bot.md`. A program's own form gets the text it shows from `bot about` (feature `bot-about-read`, #876); the window reads `bot.md` itself, being the server.
- **Secrets** (#877) are in a dialog of their own, reached from the form's Secrets row (editing only) and the bot's ` ⋯ ` menu (`secrets…`), with a `secrets` row on the card: see [Secrets › The window](secrets.md#the-window). The form's height grew by one row, for that row.
- **An ask for a secret** (#878): its notice in the chat has a cyan ` give… ` after its text while it waits; the bot's row says `asks for a secret` in the waiting colour and the card has an `asks` row per ask; the dialog (`ui/dialog/bot_ask.rs`) opens by itself in a window that shows that chat with no draft and no other dialog, and from the toast's ` go `. See [Asks](asks.md).
- **`rename`** is the small name form (a refusal shows in red in it). **`remove…`** asks `Remove bot prs? Its chat, its memory and its folder are deleted. Agents it started keep running.`; while a turn runs its button says ` stop it first ` and stops the turn, keeping the dialog open, and reads ` remove ` again once it ended: the reading where it acts saves a trip to ` stop `.

## Links

A click on an `http://` or `https://` address in a bot's answer (alone, or as a routine run's answer) does what the window can do (#863):

- **A local window with a desktop** (it sent the default app in its `Hello`) opens **Open link**: who wrote it, the address on up to three rows, `goes to <host>`, and ` copy `, ` open `, ` cancel `. ` open ` (or `Enter`) opens the address in that window's browser; ` copy ` copies it; `Esc`, ` cancel ` or a click outside close it. Removing the bot closes it.
- **A remote window, the browser, an ssh session or a Linux console** copies the address at once, as before.
- **An address the window would refuse** (not ASCII, a user name before the host, an IP address spelled as a browser reads another, over 2,048 bytes, a character a URL cannot hold) is copied, with a toast that says why it is not opened.
- An address longer than the chat is cut across rows; a click on its first row opens it whole (it is found again in the answer that was clicked), a click on a later row does nothing.
- Links in a followed agent's `finished` message, in your own messages, steps, notices and errors are not clickable.

The limits and why are in [Opening in other apps › A link in a bot's answer](../opening-in-other-apps.md).
