# Layout

Code: `config.rs`, `state.rs`, `ui.rs`, `ui/`, `app/layout.rs`, `app/sidebar.rs`, `app/tab_bar.rs`, `app/interaction.rs`

- `sidebar` in `config.json` lays out the lists (wide mode only; below 90 columns the compact menu below applies to every value): `projects_on_top` (the default since 0.9.0, `config::Sidebar`'s `#[default]`, so unknown values fall back to it), `workspaces_on_top`, `side_by_side` and `tree`. One column was made the default because it gives the panes the most room; a `config.json` that names `side_by_side` keeps it (settings save the whole file, so anyone who changed any setting before 0.9.0 keeps three columns). App tests pin `side_by_side` in `new_app`, since their geometry helpers assume it; `tests/e2e/main.rs` lays out its geometry with `Sidebar::default()`, so e2e covers the shipped default.
- Side by side: projects (32), workspaces (26), pane (after one blank column). Columns are always present so PTY sizes do not jump. Borders drag to resize (double click resets), within `MIN_COLUMN_WIDTH` and `MIN_PANE_WIDTH`; `Widths::fit` squeezes them on small terminals. Widths are saved in the session. PTYs follow the drag live.
- **Compact mode** below 90 columns (`ui::layout::compact_layout`): a top bar (` ≡ `, `project › workspace › tab`, then ` ± `, ` ▤ `, ` ☐ `, ` ⌕ ` right-aligned; ` ± ` only in git workspaces and ` ▤ ` only with a project, so their slots are left of ` ☐ ` and an empty one merges into the breadcrumb; ` ☐ ` gets a muted `●` to its right, in its own slot, while TODO items are pending, see TODO panel) and a full-screen menu showing one column at a time. Every clickable item is a 3-row band (`Areas::pitch`). Both columns' areas share one rect and `Areas::shown(nav)` blanks the hidden one, so drawing and hit testing reuse the wide-mode code. With the agents section on, it is a third menu (`Nav::Agents`): ` Agents › ` at the right of the projects menu's title opens it, ` ‹ Projects ` (the same `Areas::back`) goes back, and a click on a row closes the menu on that pane.
- **The footer: the tools, then More** (`ui/layout.rs`, `ui/chrome.rs`, `app/more_menu.rs`): the buttons under the lists are of two kinds. The tools (` Issues `, ` Changes n `, ` Files `, ` TODO `, ` Worktrees `, the plugins' buttons) sit in a grid of two columns, each label at the start of its column (`ui::changes_button` puts the second at the middle of the row, or right-aligned when its label does not fit there, so a narrow column keeps the count); TODO's dot follows its label. What belongs to the app is one button, ` More ▴ `, whose menu (`MenuAction::More`) has **update to x.y.z** (or **restart to update**) while there is one, **settings**, **usage**, **processes** (the Processes dialog, see Processes), **remote…** (the Remote menu, once it is set up) and **quit**. ` Help ` keeps its own button at the right of that row, since it is how a newcomer finds the tour. ` More ▴ ` is cyan and bold while an update waits and shows a green `●` while a browser is attached or you are away on a chat app, so folding hides no notice. Why: every button cost a row in every layout (five rows for seven buttons), and the rows mixed what you use every few minutes with what you open once a week; the app's buttons alone took three. ` Worktrees ` (#256's dialog for every project) was a row of Settings › Worktrees, **worktrees on disk**, found only by those who looked there; it is a task you come back to, not a preference, so it left Settings for the footer and the icon bar (codicon `repo-forked`, after TODO). It belongs to no project, so side by side it sits in the projects column, on the row above ` More ▴ ` that was blank, and the workspaces column keeps the project's tools; both columns keep a line and two rows, so their lines stay level, and the plugins' row, when there is one, adds a row to both. Stacked and tree: the tools' two rows, ` Worktrees `, the plugins, then a second line above ` More ▴ ` (six rows, as before). In compact mode ` Worktrees ` is a band of the projects menu above ` More ▴ `'s, which it shares with ` Help `. Always shown, since the dialog also lists the worktrees of closed projects. The icon bar has the same menu behind a gear (see the icon bar). Named More because ` ≡ ` is compact mode's menu of columns. Rejected: wrapping the buttons as a row of short labels (it grows with every button), and the update as a button of its own beside ` More ▴ ` (the colour says it). Settings, usage and quit are one click further; quit only detaches.
