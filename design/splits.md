# Splits

Code: `project/split.rs`, `app/panes.rs`

- A binary tree (`Leaf` / `Split { dir, ratio, first, second }`). Right-click in a pane opens split/close/right-click-passthrough; this works even when the program captured the mouse, since almost no program uses the right button.
- A vertical divider is followed by a blank column so text does not touch it. Inactive panes are dimmed (configurable). A left click on an inactive pane only focuses it. Dividers drag like column borders.
- **Every pane keeps its minimum** (`MIN_COLS`×`MIN_ROWS`, the size a split needs on each side). `Node::min_size` is what a subtree needs along a direction: a leaf its minimum, a split along that direction both sides plus the divider's gap, a split across it the larger side. Each split hands its sides at least their minimum when its area allows it (`project::split::divide`), so a client smaller than the one the splits were made on squeezes the panes down to their minimum, never to nothing; the stored `ratio` does not change, so growing back restores the proportions. A divider drag is clamped to the same minimum of both sides, nested splits included (`Divider` carries them, `ratio_at` clamps), so the stored ratio is what the screen shows. Before (#33), the drag left 1 cell on each side, and that side could be a nested split whose panes then got 0 rows: invisible, unclickable, and the active one still took the keys.
- **A tab too small for every pane shows only its active pane** over the whole tab (`Node::has_room`, `Node::visible`), until there is room again: a phone attaching to splits made on a big screen, a narrow window. Drawing, hit testing, `App::resize` and `control` all go through `visible` (`Tab::shown`, `Tab::rect`, `Tab::pane_at`), so keys and clicks never reach a pane that is not on screen. The hidden panes keep running at their last size (their PTYs are not resized), there are no dividers, and the pane menu offers no split (`Tab::can_split`), since a new pane would be hidden too. Squeezing every pane to 1 cell was rejected: visible but useless. A single pane always shows, whatever the size.
- **Moving panes** (#107): a right-button drag from one pane onto another moves it there, keeping every program running (only the tree changes, then the PTYs are resized). The right button because it is already cornercase's in a pane (the menu), whatever the program does with the mouse; a left drag is the program's or the selection, panes have no title bar to grab (one would cost a row per pane), and modifier drags do not arrive reliably (Cmd never, Ctrl and Alt are taken by some outer terminals). So the pane menu now opens on the release, when the pointer never left the pane (`Drag::Pane` in `App::drag`, like the rows' `RowDrag`). Where it lands is `project::split::Place::at` on the pane under the pointer: the middle third in both directions swaps the two leaves (`Node::moved` keeps the shape and ratios); elsewhere the nearest edge, relative to the pane's size, removes the pane (`Node::remove`) and splits the target on that side at half (`Node::insert`, `before` for left and above). `Tab::landing` offers only moves whose tree still `has_room` (`Tab::rearranged`, which the `move-pane` request uses too); drawing (`TabView::landing`: the half the pane would take, or the whole target, on the surface with ` move here ` / ` swap `) and the drop share it. The moved pane becomes the active one. `Esc`, another button, or a release on the same pane or outside the panes cancels. Menu entries (move pane, flip a split) and Ctrl+drag were considered and left out: one gesture covers every case, flipping included (drop a pane on its neighbour's bottom edge).
- A pane can still have no room when the whole pane area is empty. Its PTY is sized at least 1×1, and mouse events for it are dropped (`pane_cell` returns `None`), never clamped into an empty range: `clamp` panics when `min > max`, which drops the event and shows the bug toast (see Panics).
