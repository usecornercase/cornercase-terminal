# Layout › Row menus, the account row and counts

Part of [Layout](../layout.md).

- **Row menus** (` ⋯ `, `ui::row_menu_button`): every group, project, workspace and tab row has a ` ⋯ ` just left of its `×` (never right of it), shown and lit like the `×` (on hover in wide mode, cyan instead of red; always, dimmed, in compact mode), that opens on press the menu a right-click opens there, anchored at the pointer (`SidebarHit::Menu`, `GroupMenu`, `WorkspaceHit::WorkspaceMenu`, `TabMenu`, `TreeHit::Menu`, all handled by the same `open_*_menu` as the right-click). The menus were only behind the right button, which nothing on screen hinted at. Three dots rather than a gear: a gear says settings, three dots say more actions for this item, the convention of the sidebars it resembles (Notion, Linear, GitHub), and `⚙` is an emoji some fonts draw two cells wide. Its column (2 cells, 4 in compact mode) is reserved in `Band::room` like the `×`'s, so hovering never reflows a row and never hides its status icon or `↓n`; names lose 2 cells. Rows narrower than `MIN_MENU_ROW_WIDTH` (20) get no ` ⋯ ` and keep those cells, since a 16-column list needs them for the names and the right-click still opens the menu. A workspace being removed shows neither.
- **The account row under each pane** (#397, `ui::split_footer`, `ui/accounts_bar.rs`, `App::pane_footer`, `App::tab_footer`): once there is an agent account and `account_switcher` is on, every pane drawn by the window gives its leaf's last row to its accounts (`split_footer(leaf, footer) -> (terminal, row)`), and the terminal is the rest, so nothing overlaps the program. Every pane gets it, an agent or not, so a PTY does not change size when an agent starts or exits. `split_footer` is the one rule: the PTY sizes (`size_tabs`, and `pane_size` for a new pane, so `+ Tab` gets no SIGWINCH on its first frame), the drawing, and every hit test on the terminal (`App::terminals`, `terminal_rect`, `footer_at`: selection, links, the wheel, ` ↓ n more `, the held, resume and restart bands, which sit on the terminal's last row, above the row) go through it, and a test fills a terminal to its size and checks it covers the leaf but its last row. A leaf keeps `MIN_ROWS` (3), so a terminal keeps 2 rows; raising it would have changed `has_room` for windows without accounts. A tab sized by a view, and `surface` frames, have no row: another UI draws its own from `PaneInfo.accounts`. The row is one line on the terminal's background with no separator: the buttons are `fg(muted)`, lit only under the pointer (cyan, `button_style`), DIM while the pane is dimmed as inactive (`split && !active && dim_inactive`, the pane's own rule) or while another program is in front (`AccountRow::blocked`); a flow's seat, a `custom` account, or a pane still being set up or resuming keep their buttons lit and disable the menu's entries, with a note saying why, since telling those apart reads the process's environment, too much for every frame. **A gesture that starts on the row belongs to the row** (`Drag::Footer`): no byte reaches the PTY, press or release, no selection starts, the pane is not focused (a click acts on that pane, as the sidebar's row buttons do), and a pill acts on release like every row; a right click there still opens the pane's menu, even when the program takes right clicks. A program's drag that started in the terminal and ends on the row still gets its release. It replaced a status bar along the bottom of the window (#379, #396) that chose what new tabs got. Once choosing an account acts on a pane (restarting its agent, or its shell), a bar had to follow the focused pane, which names one pane's account, changes as focus moves and hides that two panes side by side are on two accounts; a row per pane names every pane's accounts at once and acts on the pane it sits under, whatever has focus. It costs a row per stacked pane, which `account_switcher` turns off (the panes' menus keep **account ›**). A first version of the bar, a row of the sidebar's footer, was hard to find. Without an account the window is laid out and drawn exactly as before. See Accounts and profiles.
- **Counts** (`counts` in `config.json`, Settings → UI, on by default): a project row ends with its number of workspaces, `(n)`, and a collapsed group with its number of projects; off, neither shows (`View::counts`), for those who find the numbers noise. One switch for both, since they read the same.
