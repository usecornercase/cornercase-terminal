# Layout › The agents section

Part of [Layout](../layout.md).

- **Agents section** (`agents_section` in `config.json`, Settings → UI, off by default): one list of every pane running Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI or opencode, in every project, at the bottom of the sidebar, so an agent that needs you is one click away whatever project is shown; the status icons on project rows said that something needed you, not which agent, and finding it meant three clicks. Off by default so the sidebar of someone who runs no agents stays as it was; off, every layout is drawn exactly as before (the existing snapshots did not change). `ui::full_layout` takes the switch (`layout_with` is the same without it, for the callers that never show it). It sits below the lists and above the footer, after a line that drags (`Border::Agents`; `Widths::agents` = its rows, title included, saved in the session; `None` = a third of the room; at least `MIN_STACK_SECTION`, and the lists keep theirs: `ui::layout::split_agents` with a `keep` of one section for side by side and the tree, two and their line when stacked). In side by side it takes the bottom of the projects column; in the one-column layouts `ui::layout::one_column` cuts it off the room above the footer, so stacked and tree share it, and `Widths::stacked_dragged` takes the switch so the stack line stops at the section. The drag stores what the layout made of the pointer (`App::agents_dragged` lays out again with the wanted rows and keeps the height it got), so every layout clamps it the same way. Rows (`ui::AgentEntry`, `ui::agent_rows`: two lines each, `pitch.max(2)`, no sticky button, scrolled by item like the other lists) are in sidebar order (projects from `project::sidebar_order`, then workspaces, tabs and panes) shaped as a tree (see the next bullet), not most urgent first: rows that move are hard to click, and the icon already says which one waits. One row per pane, so a click focuses that pane in a split tab. The first line is the status icon and `project › workspace` (the workspace only when the project has more than one, `› default` says nothing); the second is the agent's kind, then the model and the context percentage as the tab line shows them (the same `model` and `context` switches; `ui::workspaces::draw_details` takes the kind as a lead that is never cut). Memory is left out: it is only measured for panes on screen (see Memory), and widening that gate would run a thread every 2 s per agent off screen. A flow member drawn under its run shows its role instead of the place, and the run's other rows add ` · role` after it (see the next bullet). A narrow column cuts the model first, then drops it, then drops the role (kept while 4 cells of it fit, so it goes before the workspace is cut), then drops the workspace (kept while 4 cells of it fit), then cuts the project; the icon stays. The pane in focus gets the surface and the `▌`. A press grabs the row (`Grab::Agent`), a release on it jumps (`App::jump_to_pane`: `goto`, which unfolds in the tree and closes the compact menu, then `Tab::focus`); there is no drag to reorder, since the order is computed. Each row of this computer has a ` × ` (`ui::agent_close`, the other rows' close button: on hover in wide mode, red; always and dimmed in compact mode, on the middle row of the band) that opens the Close agent confirmation on press (`App::ask_close_agent`), with no drag or jump. Its column is reserved on every row (3 cells, 5 in compact mode) so hovering never moves the text, and the tags fit in what is left; rows narrower than `MIN_MENU_ROW_WIDTH` (20) get none and keep the cells, the right-click menu still offering `close agent`. A right-click opens a menu with **mark as pending review** or **mark as reviewed** for that pane (see Agent status), then **collapse** or **expand** on a row that has children (see the agents tree's folding), then **view context** (see Context use › What fills the context) and **close agent**. No agents shows `no agents running` rather than hiding the section, so the lists do not jump when the first agent starts. Every agent with an `agents::activity::Pane` status is listed, including launch-only and custom agents that emit OSC 7501.
- **Closing an agent** (#399, `agents::closing`, `app/close_agent.rs`, `app/close_agent/`): once an agent is done, getting rid of it meant jumping to its pane and finding its tab or workspace, project by project, and with 6 or 7 tasks in parallel that is the common case. The row's ` × ` and the menu's **close agent** open one confirmation, **Close agent**, and nothing stops before it is confirmed. It always asks, in every layout, unlike a tab's ` × ` in wide mode: a list you are not looking at is easy to misclick. **The unit is the tab, not the pane.** Closing only the agent's pane was rejected: it leaves the tab, the worktree and the helpers behind, which is what the user is trying to be rid of, and a script asking for it has `close --pane`. What closes depends on where the agent lives (`App::agent_close_plan`, the one place that decides, shared with `close --agent`):
  - **The unit is the tab, and nothing on disk goes** (#438): the agent's tab and the tabs of its descendants in that workspace, at any depth (a child in the workspace of a child outside it counts), whatever the workspace is: the main checkout, a plain workspace, a worktree of its own or a flow run's. Other tabs stay, and the workspace, its worktree and its branch are as they were. A tab that holds other panes closes whole and the confirmation names them (`also stops: sleep, in the same tab`), since closing half a tab would be a surprise. Why removal left: ` × ` and **close agent** read like the agent's own close, yet in a worktree of its own they used to remove the workspace and its folder, two buttons with nearly the same name that did very different things, and the destructive one sat on a list you are not looking at. Removing belongs to the workspace's ` × ` and the Worktrees dialog, where the row says what goes. The rule is #430's: the ` × ` is for an agent that is done, so it is closed for good and never saved for `▶`.
  - **A flow member** closes its whole team: if any pane that would close is a member of the run in the agent's workspace (the agent itself, a descendant, or a pane in a tab that closes), every live member becomes a root and the closing set is computed again (`agents::closing::closing`, one recomputation, since a workspace has one run). The run then ends the way every run does once none of its panes is left (`App::forget_runs`); nothing is removed, so the run's workspace, its role worktrees and its run folder stay. Closing one seat was rejected: it leaves a run without its orchestrator or a role. An agent in the run's workspace that is not a member and whose closing set reaches no member (a helper a coder started) closes alone and the run goes on.
  - **Children** are the agents it started (see the tree). Those in the same workspace close with it (its job's helpers); those it started elsewhere (`start --worktree`) are independent jobs and keep running, named in the confirmation and drawn as roots afterwards. Closing them too was rejected: it would stop work the user never pointed at.
  - **The confirmation** is the plain `ui::Update` (` close ` / ` cancel `, no git check): its heading says what closes and that nothing is deleted from disk, and the notes name the run's team when it ends, what else stops and what keeps running.
  - **Re-check on confirm**: the plan is computed again when the button is pressed, and if it grew (a new tab or child agent in the workspace, or the run) the confirmation is shown again with the new set instead of closing: confirming a list must close that list.
  - **Other choices**: a ` ⋯ ` on agent rows was left out (the right-click menu exists and the row has little room); closing several agents at once and keys-menu entries were left out. `cornercase close --agent` is the same rule for scripts (see Arranging the session).
- **The agents section is a tree** (#347): an agent that starts others (`cornercase start`, `new-tab`, `split`, or a flow's orchestrator) left its children scattered through the flat list, so which agent worked for which could not be seen. A pane now remembers the pane that started it (`Term::parent`, see Commands for scripts and agents), and each child is drawn right under its parent, indented. The order was flat on purpose, since rows that move are hard to click; the tree keeps that: rows move only when an agent starts or closes, as they already did, never by status.
  - **Order** (`agents::tree::order`, pure, no `App` nor `ui`): it takes the rows in sidebar order with their parent and walks them depth first, roots and each node's children in sidebar order. `App::agent_tree` builds the rows and `App::agent_places` is that order without the shape, so the view, hit testing, the keys menu's `a` and the bots' `/status` share it. A parent counts only while its pane exists, still runs the agent that started the child (#717) and is listed (`App::live_parent`, then the tree): a parent that is a shell or an agent cornercase cannot read, closed, or the pane itself, leaves the child a root in its sidebar place. With A → B → C, closing B makes C a root, not a child of A: one rule is easier to explain than climbing to the nearest listed ancestor. A cycle (a hand-edited session) cannot hang the walk: what the roots did not reach is taken as roots, in sidebar order. A child in another project or workspace (`start --worktree`) still hangs under its parent, and its first line names its own `project › workspace`. `order` also takes the folded pane ids (Folding below): `App::agent_tree` is the whole tree (no folds), which the keys menu's `a`, the bots' `/status`, the TODO send menu and the sounds share, and `App::shown_agents` is what the section draws, hit-tests and scrolls.
  - **Drawing**: each level takes 2 cells between the `▌` and the status icon, which moves to column `2 + 2·d`; the second line moves with it. A row at depth d has `├` (a sibling follows) or `└` (the last) in column `2·d` on its middle line, `│` there on its other lines when a sibling follows, and `│` in column `2·k` for each ancestor at level k that is not the last of its siblings (`tree::Node`'s `depth`, `last`, `rails`). It reuses the workspace list's tab guides (`draw_guide`, the line colour, blank cells only), so roots draw exactly as before and the snapshots without parents did not change. Compact mode draws the same on its 3-row bands, `├`/`└` on the middle row. Depth is capped at 3, and at 1 below `MIN_MENU_ROW_WIDTH` (20) columns, since each level costs 2 of about 24 cells; deeper rows draw at the cap and keep the guides of the levels shown. The guides are worked out on the depth drawn, not the real one (`tree::shape`, a pass over the capped depths after the walk): a row squashed to its parent's level counts as that parent's sibling, so its parent gets `├` and the squashed row the `└`, and two `└` never come one under the other. Row heights do not change, so `agent_rows`, hit testing and scrolling are as before. Sorting by status was left out.
  - **A run's team is named by its roles** (#378): a flow's members share one workspace, so their rows read the same (`web-shop › fix/empty-address` five times), and two members on one agent could not be told apart. A member drawn under a pane of its own run shows only its role (`coder`, `reviewer`), cut at the end. The role comes from `App::seat` (`Run::role_of` over `App::runs`), never from the tab's name, so renaming a tab changes nothing and a tab called `coder` outside a run has no role; the seat belongs to the pane, so another agent started in it keeps it. The rule (`App::seated`, in `agents_view`): the row's depth is above 0, its own seat and its parent's seat are in the same run (both `Some`: comparing two `Option`s would make a non-member under a non-member match) and its role is not `flows::ORCHESTRATOR`. A row at depth above 0 always hangs under `term.parent`, so `agents::tree` did not change. The orchestrator always keeps its place and adds ` · orchestrator` when the column has room; a member that is a root (its orchestrator's agent exited or its tab closed) shows its place and then its role, since nothing above it names the run; an agent a member started is not a member and looks as before. Fitting: the place first, exactly as before, and the role takes what is left (` · ` muted, the role in the row's colour), cut at the end and dropped below 4 cells (`MIN_ROLE_WIDTH`). Row heights and guides do not change, so hit testing and scrolling are as they were and no existing picture moved. `ui` only gets a string and a bool (`AgentEntry::role`, `place`), never `flows`. Rejected: the role before the workspace in a narrow column (the place is what tells two runs of one project apart) and the tab's name as the role.
  - **Folding (#383)**: a flow's run is five rows of two lines, and with a couple of orchestrators the team fills the section, so the agent you want scrolls under `↓ n more`. An agent row that has children gets an arrow, as the workspaces list and the tree do for workspaces (#294). The fold is `Term::collapsed`, like `parent` a flag of the pane: shared by every client, kept until the pane closes (an agent change does not clear it), saved (`PaneState::collapsed`, left out when false, so `VERSION` stays 5 and the v5 fixture is unchanged; the flag is on the parent itself, so a restore needs no id mapping) and expanded for every new pane, so nothing changes for anyone who never clicks.
    - **The walk**: `tree::order` leaves a folded node's descendants out of the rows it walks but marks them visited (else the second loop would take them for roots), and records them, every level, in `Node::hidden`. It does this before `shape`, so `last` and `rails` are worked out on what is drawn: with the depth cap, a folded row at the cap has its squashed children gone, and its `├` becomes `└`. `Node::has_children` comes from the walk (the unvisited children), so a cycle's back edge gives no arrow. Folds hide rows only: `agent_places` and `subagent_panes` keep the whole tree, so a fold changes no notification, sound, key or wait.
    - **The arrow** (`▾` expanded, `▸` collapsed, muted) is written after the row, like the guides, into the blank cell between the guide and the status icon (column `2·depth + 1`), so no row moves. The target is the lead (`ui::agent_fold`, from the row's first cell to the icon, the row's full height: a compact band's three rows), shared by drawing and hit testing. A press on it grabs the row with `RowDrag::fold`, and the release folds instead of jumping; anywhere else on the row the release jumps as before. The row menu adds **collapse** or **expand** (`MenuAction::AgentFold`) for touch screens, where one cell is a small target. A narrow column's depth is the capped one on both sides (`agent_row_at` uses `MIN_MENU_ROW_WIDTH` as `agents_view` does).
    - **A collapsed row** keeps its status icon and shows, right-aligned, the `attention` of the hidden agents (waiting, failed, pending, done; working ones say nothing) and `+n`, the number hidden at every level, in that order through `Tags::fit`, which drops the last tag first: in a narrow row `+n` goes before the icon, since an agent that needs you must never hide behind a fold. The tags are fitted before the place, so the place is cut first. The row is also the nearest shown ancestor of a hidden focused pane: it takes the `▌` and the surface, as `ui::tree_active_row` does in the tree.
    - **Focus**: collapsing the parent of the focused pane leaves it focused, and the rail moves up to the parent. Moving the focus to a hidden pane opens the way: `follow` compares the focused pane with the last one (`Scrolls::followed_pane`, not part of `Focus`, which would make a full-screen file exit on pane changes inside a tab) and `reveal_agent` expands the listed ancestors, so `a`, a notification click or ` go `, `focus --pane`, the bots' buttons and the agents-section click all land on a visible row. The first follow (`Scrolls::pane_followed` still false, rather than no previous pane: a focus that lands on a pane after none was open still reveals) after a restore unfolds nothing, so saved folds survive, as for projects. A pane started with `--focus`, or a click into a hidden pane, opens the way too: the focus rule wins over "a child that starts under a collapsed parent stays hidden", which otherwise holds and only raises `+n` (collapsing an orchestrator means "I do not want to see its team"). An unfold made while drawing is not a traced step, so it marks the watch snapshot changed itself; every other fold is traced and `refreshed()` is untouched. A view's focus (`--view`) never touches folds, as for `show_to`.
    - **Scroll**: a fold clamps the offset (`set_agent_fold`), so the section never shows an empty band under `↓ n more`, and expanding does not jump back to a stale offset.
    - **Scripts**: `collapse` and `expand` take a pane (`Item::Pane`; an unknown pane is `not-found`, a pane that is no listed agent `conflict`), setting the flag whatever it was. The feature `agent-folds` tells a client the item exists (an older server refuses it as `malformed`, and the CLI checks the feature before sending `--pane`, as for `todo-panel`). `PaneInfo.collapsed` is left out when false, so the v1 fixtures stay as they are, `(collapsed)` marks the pane in `status`, and `status --panes` gets no column and there is no event. `CONTRACT` and the stream versions stay 1.
    - **A machine's rows fold there** (#663, `app/machine_links/agents.rs`): the fold of a machine's agent is that pane's `collapsed` flag on the machine, read from its snapshot, and the arrow and the menu's **collapse** or **expand** send that machine `collapse` or `expand` with `Item::Pane` and the snapshot's `boot` over the relay (Remote windows › Starting work on a machine › The relay), the request its own scripts use. So this window, the machine's window and its scripts fold one team the same way, as the clients of one server do. Why it goes there while a machine's workspace folds here in memory: Layout › A machine's rows and its card.
      - **Not optimistic**: the row keeps drawing the snapshot until the next one, which the machine's `watch` pushes once the flag changes; a guess drawn here would have to be undone when the machine refuses or another client folds it back. Two clicks before it comes send the same request twice, which sets the flag whatever it was.
      - **Fixed at the press**: the grab (`RemoteAt::Agent`) and the menu's action carry a `PaneAim`, the link, the snapshot's `era`, the pane's id there and whether the row drew `▾`, so a snapshot that arrives between the press and the release cannot turn a collapse into an expand. On sending it is checked again, in this order: still `linked` (else `vps unreachable`), the same `era` (`vps restarted`), the pane still in the snapshot (`that agent is no longer on vps`), each with `nothing was sent`. A machine that is not linked keeps its arrow, dimmed, and its menu starts with the greyed reason and greys **collapse**, as a machine's workspace menu does.
      - **The answer**: accepted says nothing; `stale-boot` says `vps restarted; nothing was changed there`, any other refusal or an ssh failure `could not collapse the agent on vps: <reason>`, without the destination, the toasts a machine's workspace actions use. The cap is 15 s.
      - **No reveal towards the machine**: nothing here sends `expand` on its own: showing a machine's tab (a click on its row, ` go `, `focus-machine`) unfolds nothing there. The focus on the machine is the machine's; when it moves to a hidden pane the machine reveals it and the next snapshot draws it.
      - **Scroll**: each snapshot of a machine clamps the section's offset, as a fold here does, since a fold there, an agent that ends or a script can change the rows drawn; only while the section is on, so a snapshot does not walk every machine's rows for a list nobody draws. A stale offset left while it was off is clamped where it is used (`Rows`).
    - **Left out**: a flow's orchestrator starting collapsed, collapse all and expand all, sorting by status, a keys menu entry, and listing an agent's in-process subagents (#358) as rows.
  - **The parent belongs to the pane, the link to the agent** (#717): when a child's agent exits and another one is started in that pane, the pane keeps its own parent, but the other end is the agent that started it: a new conversation in the parent's pane leaves the child a root, and a flow's members stay under their orchestrator's pane. Close agent reads the live parents, so it never closes an earlier agent's children. Changing a parent by hand was left out.
- **A machine's agents** (#663, `app/machine_links/agents.rs`, `app/sidebar/agents.rs`): with a linked machine, its agents showed only under its workspaces (`☁ vps`), so finding the one that waits meant walking the projects, the same three clicks the section was made to save. Each pane with a status in a linked machine's snapshot is now a row too, in every layout and in the compact `Agents ›` menu. A machine with no snapshot adds none, a pane whose status this release cannot read (a newer machine) gives none, as for its tabs, and the rows of a workspace being removed there (`acting.removing`) are left out, as its tabs are. `no agents running` shows only when there is no agent here nor on any machine.
  - **Order**: each project's own agents, then those of every machine's project paired with it (in `remote_projects()` order: machines as in `config.json`, then their projects, workspaces, tabs and panes), and after every project the agents of the machines' projects with no pair here, in the order of their project rows. The section reads like the sidebar, as the local rows do.
  - **A tree per machine**: two servers count pane ids from 1, so a machine's `parent` would hang its child under a pane here or on another machine with the same id. `tree::order` takes any key, and the rows' is `(link, pane)` (`None` for this computer), so a parent, a fold and a cycle only count within one machine.
  - **Drawing**: as a local row, plus the machine's `☁ vps` at the right in the muted grey, its name cut to half the row as on its sidebar rows. The place is this computer's label of the paired project, else the machine's name for it, and the workspace follows the local rule on the machine's project. The second line comes from `PaneInfo` (kind, model, context, `compacted`, subagents), with no memory, as here; the role rule reads `PaneInfo.role` and its parent's there. A machine that is down keeps its rows dimmed, the spinner still and `<status> · seen 2 min ago` as its second line, as on its tab rows. The spinner turns, and the loop wakes for its frames, while a pane of a linked machine works (`App::remote_working` reads `PaneInfo.status`): a tab tells its most urgent pane, so a working agent beside a waiting one in a split tab would stay still in its own row.
  - **Narrow rows**: a collapsed machine row has three tags, so they drop by priority, not from the end: `+n` first, then `☁ vps`, then the attention icon, drawn `! +n ☁ vps`, so a waiting agent never hides behind a fold and the machine's name stays at the right as on the sidebar rows. They fit leaving the place at least 2 cells (`MIN_PLACE_WIDTH`), since `truncate_right` cannot cut a name to fewer and the place would run under the tags.
  - **A click** shows that tab of the machine, as a click on its tab row in the sidebar (`click_remote`), with its active pane there: focusing the row's own pane in a split tab would move the focus on the machine, which is the machine's. No focus or fold is sent and nothing unfolds there; opening the tab's view gives its panes this window's size, as any click on a machine's tab does (see Remote windows › A machine's tab). The tab is the one of the snapshot the press saw: a machine restarted before the release (its `era` moved on) shows nothing, since its ids may name another tab by then, as a fold sends nothing (`sighted_pane`). The row of the shown tab's active pane (`PaneInfo.active`), or its nearest shown ancestor on that machine, takes the `▌`, and no local row does.
  - **No ` × ` and a menu with only the fold**: a machine's tabs have no `×` yet, so its agents have none either; the column stays reserved and a press there is a click on the row. Its menu, on a row with children, holds only **collapse** or **expand**; a row without children opens none. **mark as pending review**, **view context** and **close agent** act on a pane here and read files here, so they are left out.
  - **Only the section**: `App::agent_tree` and `App::agent_places` stay this computer's, so the keys menu's `a`, the bots' `/status`, the TODO send menu, the sounds and the notices are unchanged; a machine's turns are told from its snapshots (Agent status › A machine's agents). Nothing is new on this server's socket: `status` already lists each machine's panes with their `collapsed`.
