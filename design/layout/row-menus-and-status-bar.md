# Layout › Row menus, the account status bar and counts

Part of [Layout](../layout.md).

- **Row menus** (` ⋯ `, `ui::row_menu_button`): every group, project, workspace and tab row has a ` ⋯ ` just left of its `×` (never right of it), shown and lit like the `×` (on hover in wide mode, cyan instead of red; always, dimmed, in compact mode), that opens on press the menu a right-click opens there, anchored at the pointer (`SidebarHit::Menu`, `GroupMenu`, `WorkspaceHit::WorkspaceMenu`, `TabMenu`, `TreeHit::Menu`, all handled by the same `open_*_menu` as the right-click). The menus were only behind the right button, which nothing on screen hinted at. Three dots rather than a gear: a gear says settings, three dots say more actions for this item, the convention of the sidebars it resembles (Notion, Linear, GitHub), and `⚙` is an emoji some fonts draw two cells wide. Its column (2 cells, 4 in compact mode) is reserved in `Band::room` like the `×`'s, so hovering never reflows a row and never hides its status icon or `↓n`; names lose 2 cells. Rows narrower than `MIN_MENU_ROW_WIDTH` (20) get no ` ⋯ ` and keep those cells, since a 16-column list needs them for the names and the right-click still opens the menu. A workspace being removed shows neither.
- **The account status bar** (#379, `ui/accounts_bar.rs`): once there is an agent account and `account_switcher` is on, the window's last two rows are a status bar, under the sidebar, the panes and the side panel: a line in the line colour, then a button per agent, ` claude: work ▾ ` (`Areas::accounts`), on the terminal's own background like the sidebar (a surface fill made it look stuck on). `ui::layout::window_layout` cuts those rows off the bottom (`status_split`) and lays the rest out with the usual `full_layout`, so every layout, compact included, keeps its geometry two rows shorter; `App::layout` and `ui::draw` both go through it, so the PTYs are sized without them and drawing and hit testing agree. Border drags lay out the window above the bar (`App::above_status_bar`), and a file shown full screen stops above it. In compact mode it stays below the top bar's menu, open or not, so the switcher is one tap away on a phone. A first version put the buttons on a row of the sidebar's footer above ` Usage `, with an Accounts icon in the icon bar and an ` Accounts ▾ ` row in the compact menu; trying it showed that a row of the sidebar is hard to find and moved the footer, so the bar went to the bottom, like Orca's status bar, and those entries went. Without an account the window is laid out and drawn exactly as before (`App::status_bar` is false and `window_layout` hands the whole area to `full_layout`). See Accounts and profiles.
- **Counts** (`counts` in `config.json`, Settings → UI, on by default): a project row ends with its number of workspaces, `(n)`, and a collapsed group with its number of projects; off, neither shows (`View::counts`), for those who find the numbers noise. One switch for both, since they read the same.
