# Layout › Side panels, folding, scrolling and the active row

Part of [Layout](../layout.md).

- **Side panels**: the changes, files, TODO and Flow panels share the column right of the pane (`Areas::changes`, `Widths::changes`, `Border::Changes`), one at a time (`App::side`). An open file can take the whole window instead (see Files panel). Flow (#263) shows only while the active workspace has a run (`App::panel_shown`), so a workspace without one gets the pane's width back and returning shows it again. It has no footer button or compact bar slot, since it exists only while a run does: it opens from the run workspace's menu (**show flow**), `w f`, the icon bar's Flow slot, and when a run starts from the window. Its flag is saved beside `todo` and `files` and applied after the runs are restored, so it opens only when the active workspace still has one.
- **Folding** (`Project::collapsed`, `Workspace::collapsed`, saved in the session; `App::set_collapsed` sets them for a click and for the `collapse` and `expand` requests; the tree draws both, the workspaces list draws the workspaces', #294): new projects and workspaces start unfolded. `follow` unfolds the active project and workspace, and in the tree its group, when the focus changes; a click on a row and `goto` (search) do too, for when the focus stays the same. Other layouts leave a collapsed group alone, as before. The first follow after a restore (`followed` still default) unfolds nothing, so saved folds survive. The workspaces list (side by side, stacked, compact) draws `Workspace::collapsed` with the tree's arrow, `▾`/`▸`, in the row's lead (`[▌, arrow]`, 4 cells; the name moved from 2 to 4 cells, level with the tab icons, as in the tree), always visible rather than on hover, since a click that folds should be findable and touch screens have no hover. The whole lead, the row's full height, is the target (`ui::workspaces::fold_button`, shared by drawing and hit testing; `WorkspaceHit::Fold` grabs the row with `fold: true`, so a drag from the arrow still reorders). One flag serves both views, so a fold made in the tree shows in the list. A folded workspace lists no tabs and no `+ Tab` (`TabLines::folded`, read by drawing, hit testing, drops and `follow` through `workspace_rows`); its shells keep running. With `tabs` on top there is nothing to fold: the lead keeps a blank 2-cell slot and folds are ignored there. A folded active workspace takes the surface and the `▌`, since its active tab row is hidden (the tree's nearest shown ancestor rule, only for folded rows); no `│` hangs under it. The memory gate skips a collapsed workspace's tabs in the lists too.
- **Folding a whole section** (`ui/sections.rs`, `app/sidebar/sections.rs`): a click on **Projects**, **Workspaces** or **Agents** folds that section: its rows, `+ New`, `↑ n more` and empty-list text go and the title stays. Folding a group, a project or an agent's team hides rows inside a list; nothing hid a whole list, so a sidebar could not give its height to the list in use.
  - **Drawing**: ` ▾ Title` open and ` ▸ Title` folded, the arrow muted and the title muted bold, as the old ` ▾ Bots ` title was. The arrow always shows: a click that folds should be findable and touch screens have no hover. A folded title reads like a collapsed group header: ` (n)` while `counts` is on and, right-aligned, the `agents::activity::attention` of what it hides (every project row, the shown project's workspace rows, every agent row with every agent fold open, here and on machines). The count shows only while the title keeps its first 10 cells, the length of `Workspaces` (its whole length if shorter), so no section's own title is cut to make room for it; below that the count is dropped, then the title is cut. The icon stays at every width, so an agent that needs you never hides behind a fold. Keeping the count down to a 2-cell name, as first built, was rejected: it read `P… (3)`, and the name came and went as the column widened. A workspace row's 8 cells for its `+n −n` was rejected too: it would cut `Workspaces` at one width. A project's name, the compact workspaces menu's title, does give cells to the count, down to those 10.
  - **The target** is the title's row, its full height (a 3-row band in compact mode), never ` Bots › `, ` Agents › ` or ` ‹ Projects `. `ui::section_title` gives it to both drawing and hit testing. It fills on hover like a group header, and not while a dialog is open or a drag runs. A press grabs it (`Grab::Section`), a release on it toggles; leaving it or `Esc` does nothing, a right click opens no menu, and titles take no focus. A script folding a section cancels a row or a list line being dragged, since the rows under the pointer have moved.
  - **Layouts**: every one, compact mode included (each menu's title folds its own list, the footers stay). The tree has no Workspaces title: its Projects title folds the whole tree and the Workspaces fold is kept and ignored. On the wide Bots page the page and card replace the lists, so their folds wait. A section that is not drawn (agents section off) keeps its fold.
  - **Room**: a folded section is its title row. Side by side, folded Projects leaves the column to the agents under their line, folded Agents puts its line and title above the footer, and folded Workspaces leaves that column blank at its width. Stacked, a folded top section is one row and the other takes the rest, a folded bottom one sits above the footer, and with both folded the agents section takes the room under their titles. The tree as side by side.
  - **The lines**: the stack line drags only while both lists are open, the agents line only while the agents section and a list above it are open; otherwise it is drawn but is no border (no hover, resize pointer, drag or double click). The heights saved while open come back.
  - **Nothing unfolds a section by itself**: `follow`, a search jump, `focus --pane`, a notification, ` go ` and the keys menu leave it, or every tab click would reopen a section folded on purpose; only its title and `expand --section` open it. A folded list keeps its scroll, and opening it reveals the active rows (`Scrolls::reveal_again`, which leaves a full-screen file as it is). A tour step that points into a folded section points at its title instead.
  - **Saved** in the session (see [Saved session](../saved-session.md)), shared by every client, and set by scripts with `collapse` and `expand --section` (see [Arranging the session](../commands-for-scripts-and-agents/arranging.md)).
  - **Rejected**: narrowing the folded workspaces column (columns are always there so PTY sizes do not jump, it would squeeze the footer's tools, and its border drag narrows it already); folding on press, since rows act on release; the arrow only on hover; unfolding when the focus moves; a keys menu entry (no new keys without asking, and the agents' fold added none).
- Lists scroll by item with the wheel, show ` ↑ n more` / ` ↓ n more`, and keep the `+` button sticky at the bottom. The scroll follows the active item only when it changes (`App::follow`), otherwise the wheel could never move away from it.
- The active row (project, tab, a collapsed group holding the active project, the tree's marked row) gets a surface background (palette 236, or 254 on light themes) and a cyan `▌` in the column's first cell over the row's full height (a tab's second line, a compact band), so every list shares one selection rail on the left edge. `draw_rail` and the tree lines only draw on blank cells, so text pushed left in a narrow column wins over them. Colours use palette indices so they work without truecolor; the brand blue is index 27 (`#005fff` in the xterm cube, the website's cobalt), merged pull requests keep index 99 (see Pull requests).
- **Recap is the fifth side** (#656, `Side::Recap`, `ui/recap.rs`): it shares the column, its width and its drag with Changes, Files, TODO and Flow, closes them, and `Esc` closes it like them. It has no field, so a first `Esc` closes it. See Recap.
