# Layout › A machine's rows and its card

Part of [Layout](../layout.md). What a machine has, and how its projects pair with ours, is in Remote windows › What a machine has; how its rows sit beside the model is in Hierarchy › A machine's workspaces.

- **A machine's workspaces sit under the project they pair with** (#542, `app/machines/rows.rs`, `app/machines/column.rs`, `ui/workspaces/machine.rs`): after the project's own workspaces, in the workspaces column and in the tree, each with the machine's icon and name (`☁ vps`) at the right of its row, in the muted grey, the name cut to half the row. The project's `(n)` and its attention mark count them too, so a waiting agent on the machine marks the project here. Their tabs are drawn as ours, from the snapshot: the status icon, the spinner and the details line (model, context, memory, subagents), under the same settings.
- **A machine's project with no pair here** is a project row at the end of the list, outside every group, with the same tag. Clicking it picks its first workspace; the column then lists its workspaces, and `active` here stays as it was, so choosing anything here goes back to it.
- **A machine that is down keeps its rows**, from its last snapshot, dimmed (`Modifier::DIM`) and with the spinner still (its first frame), since nothing says the agent is still at work; a tab with an agent says `<status> · seen 2 min ago` on its second line instead of its details. The rows here are never dimmed.
- **A machine's workspace has our `×` and ` ⋯ `** (#544, `app/machines/clicks.rs`, `app/machines/acting.rs`; what they send is Remote windows › Acting on a machine's workspace): on hover, and always and dimmed in compact mode, in the column and in the tree, as ours.
  - **The ` ⋯ `**, or a right-click on the row, opens a menu with only **rename workspace**, which opens the `Rename workspace` form with the name the machine gave it (its `custom_name`, else its `name`). The other items of ours (open in, ports, run setup, merge check, pull request, flow, agents, plugins) act on this computer, and greyed out for good they would only confuse, so they are left out.
  - **The `×` always asks**, in wide mode too, unlike ours: what it closes there is not seen here and cannot be undone. A plain workspace gets `Close workspace` naming the machine (`Close the workspace notes on vps?`), with the note about closed agents when the snapshot counts some; one with a worktree gets the removal dialog (see Worktrees › A machine's worktree).
  - **A machine that is not linked** keeps the buttons: its menu starts with a greyed line saying why (`vps unreachable`, or the reason the link waits on a person, as the card says) and **rename workspace** greyed, and its `×` opens nothing and says the same in a toast, with `nothing was sent`.
  - **While an action waits**, a close or a removal draws the row as ours being removed (`removing…`, dimmed, no buttons, its hits and its tabs' dropped); a rename leaves it as it is, but greys **rename workspace** in its menu, and its `×` opens nothing and says in a toast that it is still waiting, with `nothing was sent`.
- **The rest acts on nothing yet**: a machine's tabs and a machine's project with no pair here have no `×` and no ` ⋯ `, and no row has `+ Tab` or a drag: a click where our rows have a button picks the row; a right-click on them opens no menu. A workspace of ours dropped on a machine's rows lands after our own last one, in the column and in the tree: both drops read every drawn row, with the scroll that draws them, and end our block at the first of the machine's rows (`workspace_drop` with `TabLines::ours`, `tree_workspace_drop`), so the landing line stays under the pointer and never promises a move `move_before` would refuse. Reading only our rows instead would put the pointer on another row once the machine's rows scroll the column, since `Rows` clamps the scroll to the rows it has. A hit that does nothing is not drawn: starting work there comes with #543. Folding works, here only and in memory, keyed by the machine's name and the workspace's path, so it survives the machine's restart and not this server's. It is not over the socket: the `collapsed` of a workspace under `machines` in `status` is the machine's own, and folding a machine's row from a script is left for an issue of its own, since it would need a new request.
- **A click picks the workspace or tab** (`App::shown_remote()`): its row takes the active rail, and the pane area shows that workspace's active tab as it is on the machine, streamed by this server (#545, `ui/remote_tab.rs`, see Remote windows › A machine's tab), drawn like a tab here. The rounded card (`ui/remote_card.rs`, `app/machines/card.rs`) is what shows instead while there is nothing to draw, and the notice over the tab while it is down:
  - Until the first frame arrives: `runs on vps`, then `claude is waiting for you` (`working`, `done`, `failed`; the active pane's agent, else the first; `no agent there` without one) and the workspace's folder, with the machine's own home as `~` and cut at the start.
  - While the machine is down, over its last frame, dimmed (`Modifier::DIM` on every pane, the active one too, and no cursor), so what it showed stays readable and plainly not live: `vps unreachable`, `reconnecting in 4 s`, counted down each second from the link's own retry (`Down::wait`), or, when the link waits on a person (an older or missing cornercase, this computer, the same server as another), its reason; then `claude keeps working there` (`its shells keep running there` without an agent), since the link going down stops nothing there. When only the tab's stream fell and the link is up, it reads `reconnecting…`, since the stream tries again on its own.
  - `App::tick` wakes each second only while that countdown shows (`card_wake`), and the spinner keeps animating while an agent on a linked machine works (`remote_working`).
  - The empty pane's welcome is not drawn under it, as there is no local tab to talk about.
  - The active project here becomes the one it pairs with (`select_remote`), so the column lists the machine's row, and follows the pair while the workspace shows (`follow_pairing`, on each snapshot, each repository read here and each refresh): a project closed here, or a repository read that pairs it with another one, moves `active` there, else the column would mark one of our workspaces beside the card; that project's active workspace and tab stay, and the tab bar, Changes and Files keep showing them until the machine's tab can be shown (#546).
  - The tab behind the card counts as not visible (`App::visible_tab`), as under a file in full screen, so an agent there that needs you or finishes is told, and its `✓` waits until the tab shows.
  - Picking one (a click, ` go `, `focus-machine`) ends a file's full screen, and ` go ` and `focus-machine` close the compact menu, as `App::goto` does here.
- **Input goes to the machine's tab** (`app/input.rs`, `app/machines/`): while a machine's workspace is shown, keys, pastes and the mouse in the pane area reach no pane here; they go to the tab there once it is live, and before that, or while it is down, they are refused with a toast (see Remote windows › A machine's tab). The sidebar, the overlays, the prefix key, the side panels and their fields, and the tab bar still work. An action of the keys menu on the active tab or pane (split, close pane, rename, close workspace, move between panes, mark, close or reopen agents) first goes back to what is here (`acts_here` in `app/shortcuts.rs`), so it acts on what then shows, as the tab moves already did; acting on the machine's tab and panes from that menu is left for later.
- **Known limits**: a machine's project with no workspaces cannot be picked (its row has no workspace to show); `reveal_in_tree` never scrolls the tree to a machine's row, so picking one with ` go ` or `focus-machine` while it is out of sight leaves the tree where it was; with the workspaces column, `follow` only looks at the focus here, so the column is not scrolled to a machine's row out of sight either.
