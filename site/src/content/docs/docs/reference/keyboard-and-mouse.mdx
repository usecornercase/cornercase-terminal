cornercase is driven with the mouse. Out of the box it has **no keyboard shortcuts** of its own: outside of its dialogs, the changes panel's filter field, the files panel's search bar and the TODO panel's text field (and `Esc` while you drag a row), every key you press goes to the program in the active pane. That's a deliberate choice, so that nothing you type is ever taken away from your shell, your editor or your agent: `Ctrl+B`, `Ctrl+A`, `Alt` and `Option` combinations all reach them untouched.

If your hands would rather stay on the keyboard, or your terminal can't send clicks, you can turn on a **prefix key** that opens a menu of [keyboard shortcuts](#keyboard-shortcuts). It takes that one key, and only once you choose it.

## The mouse

### Columns and rows

| Gesture | Where | Does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Click | A project, workspace or tab | Makes it active. |
| Click | `+ New` | Opens a menu: **open project** (a folder) or **new group**. |
| Click | `+ New workspace`, `+ Tab` | Opens a workspace or a shell. |
| Click | A group header | Collapses or expands the group. |
| Drag | A project, group, workspace or tab | Moves it within its list; a project also into or out of a group. `Esc` cancels. See [Reorder](../../guides/projects-workspaces-tabs/#reorder). |
| Hover, then click `×` | Any project, workspace or tab row | Closes it. A project, and a workspace with its own worktree, ask first. In the [compact layout](../../guides/small-terminals/) the `×` always shows, and a tab or workspace asks first too. |
| Hover, then click `×` | A group header | Asks, then deletes the group. Its projects stay open, ungrouped. |
| Right-click, or hover and click `⋯` | A project, workspace or tab | Opens its menu, to **rename** it; a project's menu can also **move to group**. In the compact layout the `⋯` always shows. |
| Right-click, or hover and click `⋯` | A group header | Opens its menu: **add project**, **rename group**, **icon and colour**, **delete group** (which asks first). |
| Click | `▸` / `▾` in the [tree](../../guides/projects-workspaces-tabs/#the-tree) | Folds or unfolds a project or workspace. A click on the rest of its row opens it and unfolds it. |
| Wheel | The projects, workspaces or tree list | Scrolls the list. |
| Drag | The border at the right of a column | Resizes the column. |
| Double-click | A column border | Resets that column's width. |
| Drag | The line between projects and workspaces, in a stacked sidebar | Shares the height between the two lists. |
| Double-click | That line | Splits the height in half again. |
| Click | A tab in the [bar above the pane](../../guides/projects-workspaces-tabs/#tabs-above-the-pane) | Shows it. Hover it, then click `⋯` to open its menu (**rename**) or `×` to close it; right-click it to open the menu too. |
| Click | `+`, `‹`, `›` in that bar | Opens a shell; scrolls the tabs when they don't fit. |
| Drag | A tab in that bar | Moves it among its workspace's tabs. |
| Wheel | That bar | Scrolls the tabs. |
| Click | An agent in the [agents section](../../guides/projects-workspaces-tabs/#the-agents-section) | Shows that agent's project, workspace and tab, and focuses its pane. |
| Wheel | The agents section | Scrolls it. |
| Drag | The line above the agents section | Makes it taller or shorter. |
| Double-click | That line | Gives it a third of the room again. |
| Click | `Agents ›` in the [compact](../../guides/small-terminals/#the-menu) projects menu | Shows the agents. |
| Click | `Settings`, `Usage`, `Quit`, `Issues` | Opens the settings, shows your [Claude Code and Codex plan usage](../../guides/projects-workspaces-tabs/#plan-usage), detaches, opens the issue browser. |
| Click | `Changes` (or `±` in the compact bar) | Opens or closes the [changes panel](../../guides/changes/). Only in git workspaces. |
| Click | `Files` (or `▤` in the compact bar) | Opens or closes the [files panel](../../guides/files/). |
| Click | `TODO` (or `☐` in the compact bar) | Opens or closes the [TODO panel](../../guides/todo/). |
| Click | `↑ 0.2.0` or `↻ restart` at the end of the `Settings` row | Opens the [update](../../installation/#update) dialog. Only there when a new version is out. |
| Click | The search bar | Starts a [search](../../guides/search/). |

### Panes

| Gesture | Where | Does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Click | An inactive pane | Makes it active. The click isn't passed to the program. |
| Right-click | Any pane | Opens the pane menu on release: split right, split down, right-clicks, close. |
| Right-button drag | From a pane onto another pane | Near an edge, moves the pane to that side of the other one; in the middle, swaps them. See [Move and swap panes](../../guides/panes-and-splits/#move-and-swap-panes). |
| Drag | A divider between panes | Resizes the panes. |
| Double-click | A divider | Splits the space in half again. |
| Press, drag, release | Text, when the program doesn't use the mouse | Selects the text and copies it to your clipboard. |
| Hover, then click | A path to a regular file, inside or outside the workspace, in the active pane (including `~/…`) | Underlines it, then opens the file in the [files panel](../../guides/files/#paths-in-your-panes-are-links), at its line when the path has one. |
| Anything | A program that uses the mouse | Passed to the program, relative to its pane. |

Programs that use the mouse get clicks, releases, drags, movement and the wheel, depending on what they asked for, in the encoding they asked for (SGR, UTF-8 or the original X10 one). Right-clicks still open the pane menu, and right-button drags still move panes, unless you chose **send right-clicks to the pane** for that pane. See [Panes and splits](../../guides/panes-and-splits/).

### Dialogs and lists

Click buttons and rows to use them. In lists (the folder picker, the issue browser, the settings, search results) and in an update's release notes, the wheel scrolls. A right-click menu closes when you click anywhere else, and so do the search and every dialog, as its cancel button would, so whatever you typed in it is dropped. A dialog that is busy (creating a worktree, installing an update, checking a token, starting an issue) stays open until it is done.

## Keyboard shortcuts

Off by default. To turn them on, open **Settings → UI**, select **prefix key** and press the combination you want, such as `Ctrl+]`, `Alt+K` or `F12`. It must use `Ctrl` or `Alt` with a key, or be a function key; `Ctrl+C`, `Ctrl+D` and `Ctrl+Z` are refused, and the row warns you when the key you picked already means something, like `Ctrl+B` for tmux or `Ctrl+A` for the start of the line. `Backspace` there turns the shortcuts off again. The key is saved as [`prefix_key`](../configuration/#prefix_key).

Press the prefix and a menu opens at the bottom of the screen with what each next key does. Press one: it runs, and the menu closes. Keys followed by `…` open a group, such as `f` for find, whose menu takes its place, so `f` then `f` finds a file by name. There is nothing to memorise, the menu is always on screen; you can click an entry too.

| Keys after the prefix | Does |
| --- | --- |
| `n` / `p` | Shows the next / previous tab of the workspace, going round. |
| `1` … `9` | Shows that tab. |
| `]` / `[` | Shows the next / previous workspace of the project. |
| `}` / `{` | Shows the next / previous project, in the sidebar's order. |
| `←` `↑` `↓` `→` | Moves to the pane on that side, in a split tab. |
| `a` | Jumps to the next agent that needs you (`!` or `✓`), in any project. |
| `/` | Opens the [search](../../guides/search/). |
| `c` | Opens a new tab. |
| `\|` / `-` | Splits the pane right / down. |
| `x` | Closes the pane, after asking. On a tab's last pane it asks to close the tab. |
| `r` | Renames the tab. |
| `f` `f` / `f` `w` | Opens the [files panel](../../guides/files/) searching file names / the text in the files, ready to type. |
| `f` `e` | Opens or closes the files panel. |
| `f` `p` | Opens the search, like `/`. |
| `g` `d` | Opens or closes the [changes panel](../../guides/changes/), with its filter ready to type. |
| `g` `b` | Opens the changes panel and the list of branches to compare with. |
| `w` `n` / `w` `r` / `w` `x` | New workspace / renames the workspace / closes it, after asking (a worktree asks to remove it). |
| `i`, `t`, `,`, `u` | Opens the issues, the [TODO list](../../guides/todo/) (on a new item), the settings, the plan usage. |
| `q` | Quits the window. Everything keeps running, as with **Quit**. |

`Esc`, or a key the menu doesn't list, closes it without doing anything, and that key doesn't reach your program. Press the prefix twice to send the prefix itself to the program in the pane. The prefix works while the pane, the files search, the changes filter or a TODO field has the keys, but not inside a dialog, which has its own keys below.

## Keys inside cornercase's dialogs

While a dialog, the search, the settings, the issue browser, the changes panel's filter field, the files panel's search bar or a TODO field is open, the keys below drive it instead of your program.

| Where | Keys |
| --- | --- |
| Any dialog with a text field | Type, `Backspace`, paste. `Enter` confirms, `Esc` cancels. |
| New workspace | `Tab` toggles **with its own worktree**. |
| Right-click menus | `Esc` closes the menu. Other keys still go to the active pane. |
| While dragging a row or a pane | `Esc` cancels the drag. Other keys still go to the active pane. |
| Folder picker | Type to filter. `↑` `↓` select. `→` or `Tab` go into the selected folder, `←` goes up. `Enter` goes into the selected folder, or opens the folder you are in. `~` goes home and `/` to the root when the filter is empty. `Backspace` on an empty filter goes up. |
| Search | Type to search. `↑` `↓` select. `Enter` goes to the result. `Esc` closes. |
| Issue list | Type to filter. `↑` `↓` `PgUp` `PgDn` select. `Enter` reads the issue. `Tab` / `Shift+Tab` switch sources. `Esc` closes. |
| Issue | `Enter` starts it. `↑` `↓` `PgUp` `PgDn` `Space` `Home` `End` scroll. `Esc` or `←` go back. |
| Agent, place and person pickers | Type to filter. `↑` `↓` select. `Enter` picks. `Esc` goes back. |
| Changes panel filter | Type a piece of a path or a pattern: `*.test.js` keeps only those files, `!*.snap` leaves them out. `Enter` keeps the filter and gives the keys back to your program, `Esc` clears it. A click in a pane gives the keys back too. |
| Files panel search | Type to search. `↑` `↓` pick a result. `Enter` opens it. `Esc` clears the search and gives the keys back to your program. A click in a pane gives them back too and keeps the search. |
| TODO field | Type, paste. `←` `→` move the cursor, `↑` `↓` move across the lines of a long item, `Home` `End` go to its start and end, `Backspace` `Delete` delete. `Enter` saves (a new item keeps the field open for the next one), `Esc` cancels. A click outside the field saves it and gives the keys back. |
| Branch to compare with | Type to filter. `↑` `↓` select. `Enter` picks the selected branch. `Esc` closes. |
| Settings | `Enter` or `Space` change the selected row. `↑` `↓` select. `Tab` `→` / `Shift+Tab` `←` switch tabs. `Shift+↑` `Shift+↓` reorder sources. `Delete` removes a saved token. `Esc` closes. |
| Update | `↑` `↓` scroll the release notes. `Enter` presses the dialog's first button: **update**, **try again**, **restart now** or **copy command**. `Esc` closes it, except while downloading, like **cancel** or, once the update is installed, **later**. |
| Compact menu | `Esc` closes it. |
| Keys menu | The key next to an entry runs it, or opens its group. `Esc` closes it. The prefix again types it in the pane. |

## Keys in your panes

Everything else goes to the program in the active pane, encoded like a modern terminal would:

- When a program enables the **kitty keyboard protocol**, cornercase encodes every key with it, using Ghostty's encoder.
- Otherwise special keys go through Ghostty's encoder and ordinary characters through the classic encoding, so old programs keep working.
- cornercase asks your own terminal to report keys unambiguously, so combinations such as `Shift+Enter` and `Ctrl+Enter` keep their modifiers, as long as your terminal supports it.
- Pastes are wrapped in bracketed-paste markers when the program asked for them.

<Aside type="note">
Programs inside cornercase never receive key releases, even with the kitty protocol. That matters only for the rare program that reacts to them, such as some games.
</Aside>
