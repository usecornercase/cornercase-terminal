The sidebar takes 32 columns of your terminal, or about 58 with projects and workspaces side by side. When the terminal is narrower than **90 columns** (a split in your window manager, a laptop with large fonts, a phone over SSH), cornercase switches to a **compact layout**: the columns fold away and the active tab gets the whole width.

## The bar

| Part | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `≡` | Opens and closes the menu. It shows `!` when Claude Code or Codex needs you in another tab, or `✓` when it finished there; see [What your agents are doing](../projects-workspaces-tabs/#what-your-agents-are-doing). |
| `project › workspace › tab` | Where you are. Clicking it opens the menu too. |
| `±` | Opens the [changes panel](../changes/) over the whole screen. Only in git workspaces. |
| `▤` | Opens the [files panel](../files/) over the whole screen. |
| `☐` | Opens the [TODO panel](../todo/) over the whole screen. |
| `⌕` | Opens the [search](../search/). |

## The menu

The menu covers the screen and shows one column at a time. It opens on the workspaces and tabs of the active project:

<Screen
  name="compact-menu-workspaces"
  alt="The compact menu showing the workspaces of shop: main with its nvim and bash tabs, each row with its ×, a hint that more rows are below, + new workspace and issues"
/>

Click a tab to switch to it and close the menu. **`‹ projects`** goes back to the list of projects, which also holds `settings`, `usage` and `quit` (and, when a new version is out, the [update](../../installation/#update) button at the end of the `settings` row):

<Screen
  name="compact-menu-projects"
  alt="The compact menu showing the projects: shop, api, infra and notes, each with its ×, + new project, settings, usage and quit"
/>

With the [agents section](../projects-workspaces-tabs/#the-agents-section) on, **`agents ›`** at the top right of the projects list opens a third list, every Claude Code and Codex running in any project. Click one to jump to it; the menu closes on its pane. **`‹ projects`** goes back.

<Screen
  name="compact-menu-agents"
  alt="The compact menu showing the agents: shop › feat/login waiting for you with claude, Opus 5.5 at 23%; api working with codex, gpt-5.5 at 41%, marked as the agent on screen; notes idle with claude, Sonnet 5.5 at 2%; ‹ projects at the top"
/>

Click a project to see its workspaces. Everything you can do in the wide layout is here: the `+` buttons, right-click to rename, group headers to collapse, dragging to [reorder](../projects-workspaces-tabs/#reorder), the wheel to scroll. Each row is three lines tall, so it's easy to hit.

Every row shows its `×` all the time, since a touch screen has no hover. Because it is easy to tap by accident, closing a tab or a workspace asks first here, as closing a project always does. A workspace with its own worktree shows only its usual [remove](../worktrees/#remove-a-worktree) dialog.

Click the bar again or press `Esc` to close the menu.

## Switching back

The layout follows the size of your terminal as you resize it. At 90 columns or more the columns come back, with the widths you gave them. If several terminals are attached, the [one you used last](../sessions/#several-terminals-at-once) decides.

A tab split into more panes than fit shows only its active pane until there is room again; see [When the tab gets too small](../panes-and-splits/#when-the-tab-gets-too-small).
